# Bank Personal Loan Analysis & Customer Segmentation

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing banking customer data to understand customer characteristics and identify patterns associated with personal loan acceptance.

The objective is to explore customer demographics, income patterns, financial characteristics, and loan acceptance behavior to support data-driven marketing and business decisions.

## Business Objectives

- Understand customer demographics and financial characteristics.
- Analyze personal loan acceptance patterns.
- Identify customer groups with higher loan acceptance.
- Explore relationships between customer attributes and loan acceptance.
- Generate insights to support targeted marketing campaigns.

## Tools & Technologies

- **Python:** Data analysis and exploratory data analysis.
- **Pandas & NumPy:** Data manipulation and cleaning.
- **Matplotlib & Seaborn:** Statistical analysis and visualization.
- **SQL:** Data querying and analysis.
- **Microsoft Excel:** Data analysis, formulas, PivotTables, and charts.
- **Power BI:** Interactive dashboard development.
